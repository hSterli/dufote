Dufote Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026
Last updated: March 23, 2026

Dufote (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the Dufote mobile application, website, and related services.

By using Dufote, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

a. Information You Provide

  • your name, email address, and account login details;
  • trip information such as destinations, travel dates, trip type, and travel preferences;
  • packing lists, saved items, notes, checklists, and custom entries;
  • content you upload, such as images or attachments, if available in the App;
  • messages or support requests you send to us; and
  • payment-related information provided through app stores or payment processors (we do not typically store full card details ourselves).

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • device type, operating system, app version, and language;
  • IP address or approximate location inferred from IP;
  • usage data, app interactions, feature engagement, and crash diagnostics;
  • identifiers used for analytics, authentication, or fraud prevention; and
  • log information relating to your use of the service.

c. Information from Third Parties

  • authentication providers, such as Sign in with Apple or Google;
  • analytics and crash reporting providers;
  • payment processors and app store platforms; and
  • weather or travel data providers, where relevant to app functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • provide trip-planning and packing features;
  • personalize recommendations and checklists;
  • sync your data across devices, where supported;
  • improve the App, features, reliability, and user experience;
  • process purchases and subscriptions;
  • respond to customer support requests;
  • detect, prevent, and investigate misuse, fraud, or security issues;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • communicate with you about updates, service information, and, where permitted, marketing.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (UK/EEA Users)

Where applicable, we process personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • performance of a contract with you;
  • our legitimate interests in operating and improving Dufote;
  • your consent, where required;
  • compliance with legal obligations; and
  • protection of vital interests where relevant.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share your information with:

  • service providers that help us operate the App, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, customer support, and infrastructure providers;
  • payment processors and app platforms to manage purchases and subscriptions;
  • professional advisers, auditors, insurers, or legal representatives where needed;
  • authorities or regulators if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security; and
  • a buyer, investor, or successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

If you use sharing features in Dufote, information you choose to share may be visible to others according to the settings and functionality of that feature.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, how it is used, and legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your own. Where required by law, we take appropriate safeguards to protect transferred personal data, such as contractual protections or reliance on recognized adequacy mechanisms.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • request portability of your data;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with a relevant supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

9. Children’s Privacy

Dufote is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us unlawfully, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

If Dufote has a website or uses web-based services, we may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics, and performance.

Where required by law, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

11. Third-Party Services

Dufote may link to or rely on third-party services, such as app stores, sign-in providers, maps, weather data providers, payment processors, analytics tools, or customer support platforms. Those third parties may collect and process information under their own privacy policies.

12. In-App Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use analytics and crash reporting tools to understand how users interact with Dufote, diagnose problems, and improve performance. These tools may collect device information, usage patterns, and technical diagnostics.

13. Marketing Communications

Where permitted by law, we may send you updates, offers, or product news. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option in the message or contacting us.
